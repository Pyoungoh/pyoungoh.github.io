# Daily medication checklist

[Back to DoseButler](/dosebutler/en/)

See today's doses in time order and check each one off with a tap.

A progress count like "2/3 done" shows where you stand at a glance.

## What you can do

- View today's doses in time order
- Check a dose off as taken
- See when your next dose is due
- Each dose shows a status: upcoming, taken, missed, or skipped
- Skip, snooze for 10 minutes, or edit a medication from the item menu
- See a confirmation when every dose for today is done

## When to use it

Good for situations like these:

- You are not sure whether you took today's medication
- You missed the notification but already took the dose
- You want to check when the next dose is due

## Example

If three doses are scheduled today and you have taken two, DoseButler shows "2/3 done" along with the time of your next dose.
