# Medication schedule

[Back to DoseButler](/dosebutler/en/)

Set up a schedule for each medication and DoseButler applies it to reminders and your daily checklist.

Schedule medication taken several times a day, on certain weekdays, or every few days.

## What you can do

- Choose how often: daily, selected weekdays, or every N days
- Add multiple dose times per day
- Use presets for after waking, morning, noon, evening, and bedtime
- Set a custom time
- Duplicate dose times are blocked
- Changing a dose time updates upcoming doses and keeps past records

## When to use it

Good for situations like these:

- You take a medication in the morning and evening
- You take a medication on certain weekdays, like Monday, Wednesday, and Friday
- You take a medication every other day

## Example

For a medication taken every morning (after waking) and evening, choose "Daily" and add two preset times.
