# Pill organizer

[Back to DoseButler](/dosebutler/en/)

Keep each medication organized by name, dose, shape, and color.

Pause a medication you are not taking for now, and turn it back on when you need it.

## What you can do

- Add a medication name and a note
- Open a medication to see its schedules, adherence over the last 30 days, and dose history
- Enter a dose: tablet, capsule, packet, mL, or time(s)
- Pick a shape: pill, capsule, powder, liquid, injection, or other
- Tell medications apart with 10 colors
- Deactivate: stops reminders and keeps the history
- Delete: removes it from your organizer and reminders and keeps past records

## When to use it

Good for situations like these:

- You want to tell several medications apart at a glance
- You are taking a break from a medication
- You want to clean up a medication you no longer take

## Example

If you pause a medication for two weeks, deactivate it instead of deleting it. Reminders stop, and your past dose history stays.
